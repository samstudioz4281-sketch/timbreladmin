THEME
“Praise Him With Timbrel And Dancing” — Psalm 150:4

CRITERIA

  1. Group Size
    • Minimum: 8 members
    • Maximum: 16 members
  2. Age Limit
    • No age limit
    • Senior Home League members are eligible.
  3. Uniform
    • Participants must wear Salvation Army Uniform only.
    • Proper and complete uniform must be maintained throughout the performance.
  4. Music
    • Song/music track will be provided by THQ.
    • No changes or modifications to the provided music are permitted.
  5. Registration
    • All groups must register before 10 November 2026.
    • Registration fee: ₹100 per contestant.
    • No additions or changes to the participant list after final submission.
  6. Punctuality
    • All groups must report to the venue at least 30 minutes before their scheduled performance time.
  7. Decision of Judges
    • Judges’ decision will be final and binding.
  8. Participation Certificate
    • All participating groups will receive a certificate of participation.
  9. Respect for Theme
    • Performance must reflect Salvation Army values and the festival theme: “Praise Him With Timbrel And Dancing” — Psalm 150:4
“Let all things be done decently and in order.”
— 1 Corinthians 14:40

REGISTRATION PROCEDURE

  1. Select Division.
  2. Select Corps.
  3. Enter the Phone Number.
  4. Select Group Name and click Reserve Group (once Reserved is shown, go to the next step).
  5. Choose the number of contestants.
  6. Enter each contestant's name and upload their photo.
    Upload one passport-style photo per contestant: solo shot, facing the camera directly, eyes open and looking straight ahead, well lit and in focus. Photos are checked automatically for orientation, size and blurriness.
  7. Click Submit Registration.
